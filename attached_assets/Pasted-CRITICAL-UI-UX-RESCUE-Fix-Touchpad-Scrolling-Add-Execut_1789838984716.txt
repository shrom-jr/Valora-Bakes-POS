CRITICAL UI/UX RESCUE: Fix Touchpad Scrolling, Add Executive KPIs, and Compact Menu & Inventory Layouts

The current dashboard has three major flaws: two-finger touchpad scrolling fails because wheel events are blocked, Menu Management lacks summary KPIs and has bloated cards, and Inventory lacks stock health metrics and looks empty. Refactor the entire dashboard workspace immediately:

1. Fix Touchpad Two-Finger Scrolling Engine:
- The content container currently blocks touchpad wheel/scroll gestures.
- In the dashboard shell and layout components, remove any `touch-action: none`, event-interception traps, or rigid nested `overflow-hidden` wrappers on parent containers.
- The main content area must be configured for native fluid scrolling:
  - Container class: `h-[calc(100vh-4rem)] w-full overflow-y-auto overscroll-y-auto [touch-action:pan-y] p-6 lg:p-8 space-y-8`
  - Ensure standard mousewheel and touchpad two-finger scroll events bubble and trigger native vertical scrolling without requiring manual scrollbar dragging.

   - Card 4: "Cakes / Weight Items" (Count of items sold with pound/weight tiers).
