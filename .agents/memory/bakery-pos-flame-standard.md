---
name: Bakery POS flame standard
description: The approved visual direction for future Bakery POS screens and components.
---

Use velvet obsidian surfaces with high-chroma Firebase-inspired gold, blazing orange, and fiery coral. Primary hardware-like panels need visibly luminous gradient rims, and key icons need true multicolor gradient strokes rather than flat orange or gray.

**Why:** A muted amber-on-charcoal pass looked muddy and failed the requested incandescent Firebase character. The accepted direction depends on clear yellow-to-vermilion separation, bright rim lighting, and visible bloom.

**How to apply:** Extend new Bakery POS UI with the established flame spectrum, luminous rim wrappers, crisp white/off-white typography, emerald online states, and tabular monospaced POS figures. Keep disabled primary actions visibly flame-branded while preserving disabled behavior.
